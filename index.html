How I think

Impact Canvas, but for products.

Every case study makes the thinking visible instead of presenting only a polished final screen.

Problem-first

Make the reasoning visible.

Start with the problem and desired outcome. Expose assumptions. Then show the trade-offs behind the product decision.

01 · PROBLEM

What is broken?

User, context, pain point and evidence — separating symptoms from the underlying problem.

02 · OUTCOME

What should become true?

Define the user and business outcome before jumping to features.

03 · INSIGHT

What did evidence tell us?

Customer conversations, operational data, product signals and stakeholder input.

04 · DECISION

What did we choose — and why?

Impact, effort, risk, dependencies and reversibility made explicit.

05 · SHIP

How did it become real?

Requirements → solution design → implementation → QA → rollout → adoption.

06 · IMPACT

Did it work?

Close the loop with outcomes, learnings and the next iteration.
