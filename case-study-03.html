01 · Context

Enterprise logistics, from inbound to customs.

FarEye is an enterprise logistics SaaS platform. The clients I work with run inbound, sorting, routing, delivery, returns and customs operations on it, across Southeast Asia and the GCC. I joined as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in 2021 and have been a Solution Engineer since 2024.

02 · Problem

The product assumed one way of working. Customers ran another.

Enterprise operations rarely run exactly the way a product expects. Where they didn't match, the same customer issues kept coming back, recurring reporting was done by hand, and feedback took too long to reach a release.

My job: find where the product gets in the way of the work, and get it fixed with Product and Engineering.
03 · What I did

Document, log the gaps, specify, enable.

Document

Customer SOPs, end to end

Documented how clients run inbound, sorting, routing, delivery, returns and customs.

Log

A gap register

Turned the differences between those workflows and the product into a gap register.

Specify

Proposals with acceptance criteria

Wrote recurring customer issues up as proposals and acceptance criteria, as roadmap input for Product and Engineering.

Enable

Training for every change

Built a training package (video, slides, reference guides) for every change shipped, covering context, procedure, exceptions and escalation paths.

05 · Hardest part

Connecting physical sorting machines to the platform, from scratch.

The sorting machines and the platform had to agree on every parcel. I designed the integration layer between them, at the boundary where hardware meets software.
